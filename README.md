# The Lovász Local Lemma and two applications in Lean 4

Author and maintainer: **Juan Pablo Traverso Gianini**. Licence: Apache-2.0 (see `LICENSE`).

Let `A 0, …, A (n-1)` be events in a probability space and `Γ` a graph on the indices. `Γ` is a
*dependency graph* (`LovaszLocalLemma.IsDependencyGraph`) if each `A i` is mutually independent of
the family of events `A j` with `j ≠ i` not adjacent to `i`: the σ-algebra generated by `A i` is
independent (Mathlib's `ProbabilityTheory.Indep`) of the σ-algebra generated by those events. All
statements are in the namespace `LovaszLocalLemma`:

1. `lll`, **the Lovász Local Lemma, general form** (Erdős–Lovász 1975; Spencer 1977). If
   `0 ≤ x i < 1` and `P(A i) ≤ x i ∏_{j ∼ i} (1 − x j)` for every `i`, then
   `P(⋂ i, (A i)ᶜ) ≥ ∏ i, (1 − x i)`.
2. `lll_symmetric`, **the symmetric Local Lemma.** If every event has probability at most `p`,
   every vertex of `Γ` has degree at most `d`, and `e p (d + 1) ≤ 1`, then with positive
   probability none of the events occurs.
3. `ramsey_lower`, **Spencer's Ramsey lower bound (1977).** If
   `2 e (C(k,2) C(n,k-2) + 1) ≤ 2^C(k,2)`, then some graph on `n` vertices has neither a clique nor
   an independent set of size `k`, so `R(k,k) > n` (for example `R(10,10) > 96`).
4. `propertyB`, **Erdős–Lovász (1975).** A `k`-uniform hypergraph in which every edge meets at most
   `d` other edges, with `e (d + 1) ≤ 2^(k-1)`, has a 2-colouring with no monochromatic edge.
5. `propertyB_regular`: for `k ≥ 9`, a `k`-uniform hypergraph in which every vertex lies in at
   most `k` edges is 2-colourable.

Probabilities are `Measure.real` of a probability measure; the hypergraph statements need no
finiteness of the vertex type. The definition `IsDependencyGraph` is stated in `Challenge.lean`;
`comparator.json` selects only the theorems, and the definition enters the check only as part of
the theorem statements that use it. Mathlib has independence and product measures but not the
Local Lemma.

## How the proofs go

* **General Local Lemma.** By strong induction on `|S|`, for `i ∉ S` and
  `C_S = ⋂_{j ∈ S} (A j)ᶜ`, `P(A i ∩ C_S) ≤ x i · P(C_S)`. Split `S` into the neighbours and the
  non-neighbours of `i`; the numerator is bounded through the non-neighbours using independence,
  and the denominator from below by the chain rule and the induction hypothesis. Taking `S` to be
  all indices and chaining gives `P(⋂ (A i)ᶜ) ≥ ∏ (1 − x i)`.
* **Symmetric form.** Take `x i = 1/(d + 2)` and use `(1 + 1/(d+1))^(d+1) ≤ e`.
* **Random colourings** (`Coin.lean`). Independent fair coins on `ι → Bool` (Mathlib's product
  measures, `iIndepFun_pi`). Events depending on disjoint sets of coordinates form a dependency
  graph, and a set `E` of coordinates is monochromatic with probability at most `2 · 2^(−|E|)`.
* **Spencer.** Colour the pairs of `Fin n`; the bad events are the monochromatic `k`-sets, two of
  them dependent only when they share at least two vertices, which bounds the degree by
  `C(k,2) C(n,k-2)`.
* **Property B.** Colour the vertices occurring in the hypergraph; for the regular case, each edge
  meets at most `k(k − 1)` others and `e (k(k−1) + 1) ≤ 2^(k−1)` for `k ≥ 9`.
